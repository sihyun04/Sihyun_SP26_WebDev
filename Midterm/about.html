About

I am an Industrial Design student at KAIST minoring in Computer Science. My practice focuses on interactive media, installation, digital storytelling, and user experience design.

I am especially interested in creating immersive experiences that combine visual communication, physical interaction, and emotional engagement. Through my projects, I explore how design can make abstract ideas tangible and memorable.

My work spans media art, projection mapping, UI/UX design, and research-based design practice.
